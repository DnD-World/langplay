## What is Langplay?

Imagine a robot helper that answers questions. Langplay lets you build that helper out of blocks, like LEGO. Each block does one job, and the helper does the jobs in order.

## The blocks

- 📝 **Question block** — puts your question into a sentence.
- 🧠 **Thinking block** — the robot writes an answer.
- 🔎 **Tool block** — the robot looks something up, or uses a calculator, so it doesn't guess.
- 📚 **Book block** — the robot reads *your* files and says which page it used.
- 🔀 **Signpost block** — the robot picks which way to go, like "money question" or "broken app".
- 🧐 **Teacher block** — checks the answer and says "try again!" if it's not good yet.
- 🏁 **Finish block** — the robot gives you the final answer.

## Peek inside

After you press **Run**, you can click any block and see exactly what the robot was told and what it said back. No secrets!

## Where does the robot's brain live?

- At first, a free robot on the internet answers (but it gets tired after a few questions).
- You can give Langplay a free "key" to a bigger robot — the app shows you how in 2 minutes.
- Or download a small robot brain that lives right on your computer, no internet needed.

## Games

There are 12 little missions. Do them and you earn points, coins and new ranks, all the way up to *LangGraph Legend*.

## Make your own tool

When your helper works, press **Spin out** and it becomes its own little app you can share — a web page, a button on a website, or something grown-up programmers can use.
